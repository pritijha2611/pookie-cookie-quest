Pookie's Cookie Quest

Run through the forest, collect cookies and conquer academic challenges!

🎮 How to Play
• Use ↑ Arrow Up or Space to jump.
• You can also use the big JUMP button.
• Collect as many 🍪 cookies as possible.
• Jump over hurdles to trigger academic questions.
• Correct answers earn cookies and points.
• Wrong answers cost one life.
• If the timer reaches zero, the question disappears and you lose one life.
• Each level has exactly 10 questions.
• There are 3 lives per game.
